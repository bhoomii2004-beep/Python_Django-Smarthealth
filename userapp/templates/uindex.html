Smart Health With RFID card

One Card, Instant Patient Details, Better Healthcare Experience.!

The Smart Health System with RFID Card is an IoT-based healthcare solution designed to make patient management faster, smarter, and more accurate. Each patient is provided with a unique RFID card that securely stores identification and medical information. When the card is scanned at the hospital, patient details, medical history, and appointments are retrieved instantly. This reduces paperwork, saves time, minimizes errors, and improves the overall efficiency of hospital services. The system ensures quick patient identification, smooth record handling, and better healthcare delivery through smart technology.
